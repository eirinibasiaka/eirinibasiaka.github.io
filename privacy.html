Privacy Policy

Sway: Tilt the Box · last updated 18 September 2026

Sway is made by Eirini Basiaka, an individual developer based in Greece. This page explains what the game does with information, in plain terms. It is short because the game does very little.

What Sway itself collects

Nothing that leaves your device. The game stores which levels you have reached and how many attempts you have made, using Apple's standard on-device storage, and it keeps a local log of play sessions on the device. None of it is uploaded, and there is no account, no login and no server of ours to send it to. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the advertising provider collects

Sway shows one kind of advertisement: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch in exchange for one extra tilt. You are never required to watch one. These come from Google AdMob, and Google, as an independent controller, may collect:

IdentifiersYour device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
Usage dataAdvertising data and interactions with the advertisement.
DiagnosticsCrash, performance and other diagnostic data.

Google's own policy governs that data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

Tracking. iOS asks once whether Sway may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the game works exactly the same and the advertisements simply become less personalised. You can change the answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The first time the game is about to show an advertisement, you are asked for consent under the GDPR. You can revisit and change that choice at any time from the privacy option in the game's settings screen.

Purchases

Sway offers one purchase, which removes advertisements. It is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, and we receive no information about you from the transaction beyond whether the purchase is active on your device.

Children

Sway is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or erased. Because Sway holds none of it — everything the game stores stays on your own device — such a request is answered by pointing you to Google for the advertising data described above, or by deleting the app, which removes everything the game itself keeps. You are also entitled to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority; in Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
