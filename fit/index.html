Place the hand you were dealt.

A block puzzle for iPhone, played in levels. Each hand deals three pieces and you place them in the order they came; clear every marked cell before the hands run out.

Support. A question, a bug, or a purchase that did not restore? Write to eirinibasiaka.dev@gmail.com and say which iPhone and which level. Purchases are restored from Settings, "Restore purchases". Your progress stays on your iPhone; there is no account.
