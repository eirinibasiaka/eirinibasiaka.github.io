Fit - Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026.

Fit is a block puzzle game for iPhone, published by Eirini Basiaka. You can reach me about anything on this page at eirinibasiaka.dev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Fit has no account, no login and no server of mine. I do not receive your name, your email address or your game progress.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • The only thing that collects anything is the advertising SDK, and only around a rewarded video you chose to watch.
  • You decide whether that advertising is personalised, and you can change your mind at any time from inside the game.

What stays on your phone

Fit keeps three things in your iPhone's own app storage: the furthest level you have reached, whether sound is on, and whether haptics are on. They never leave the device, they are not backed up to any service of mine, and deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Fit shows one kind of advertisement: a rewarded video that you may choose to watch when a board is stuck and its free relief is already spent. There are no banners, no interstitials and nothing that plays without you asking for it. The videos are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK; its handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

When that SDK runs, it may collect the categories the app declares in its Apple privacy manifest:

  • Device ID - an advertising identifier, used for third-party advertising, for measuring the app's own advertising, and for analytics. This is the one category also used for tracking, and it is the one the tracking permission prompt is about.
  • Advertising data - which advertisements were shown and interacted with.
  • Product interaction - that an advertisement was requested, shown or closed.
  • Crash data and performance data - used for analytics and for keeping the advertising working.

Fit itself collects none of these. It has no analytics of its own, no crash reporter of its own and no network connection of its own.

Your two choices, and how to change them

Fit asks you twice, once each, and both after you have cleared the first level - never at launch:

  • A consent message, shown by Google's User Messaging Platform where the law requires one (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). It is what decides whether you are shown personalised advertising. If you are not in such a region, no message appears.
  • Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether Fit may use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Saying no is a complete answer: the game plays exactly the same, and the rewarded video still works.

You can change the first at any time. Open Settings inside Fit and press Ad privacy options - the same pill is shown wherever a consent message was required. You can change the second at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you refuse consent, no advertising SDK is started at all for that session.

Purchases

Fit offers one purchase, Remove ads, which removes the video from the relief and leaves the relief. It goes through Apple's App Store and nothing else. I never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded. There is no subscription and nothing is charged for anything else in the game.

Children

Fit is not directed at children and is not listed in the App Store's Kids Category. I do not knowingly collect anything from a child. If you believe a child has been shown something they should not have been, write to me at the address above and I will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If what the game does changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top changes too. The address of this page will not change.
