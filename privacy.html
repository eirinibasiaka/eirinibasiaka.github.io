Privacy Policy

Sway: Tilt the Box, for iPhone · and this website · last updated 22 September 2026

The short version. The game itself keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. The only outside parties are Google, which serves the optional rewarded video — and, if you consent, the advertising partners Google works with, listed inside the consent form — and Apple, which handles the one purchase. 1. What the game keeps on your phone

Sway has no account, no login and no server of ours. Everything the game itself records stays in the app's own storage on your device, is read only by the game, and is never uploaded:

ProgressThe level you have reached, how many attempts each level took, and your sound setting.
PurchaseWhether Remove Ads is owned, mirrored from Apple's answer so the game can act on it instantly.
Privacy choicesYour answers to the tracking prompt (kept by iOS) and to the consent form (kept on the device by Google's consent library, in the standard IAB format).
Play logA local file of play events — a level started or finished, a tilt made, whether it was blocked, and the times these happened. It contains no identifier of you or your device.

Why: to run the game — the legal basis is performing our side of the deal with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). How long: until you delete the app, which deletes all of it. Nothing here is required from you; the game works if you never enter anything, and you never do.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

Sway shows one kind of advertisement: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch in exchange for one extra tilt when you are one tilt short of the goal. You are never required to watch one. The videos are served by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (the consent form), which are part of the app.

What Google collects

Google, and the advertising partners it works with, may collect the following through the app, as described in Google's own disclosures:

IdentifiersYour device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. Without that permission the SDK does not receive it.
NetworkYour IP address, and an approximate location derived from it, used both to decide which consent rules apply to you and to select advertisements.
Device and appDevice model, operating system version, language, the app's identifier and version.
Ad activityWhich advertisements were requested and shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and whether you tapped it.
DiagnosticsCrash, performance and other diagnostic data about the advertising software.

Why: to select, show and measure the advertisements that fund the free game; to detect fraud and invalid traffic; and, only where you have agreed, to personalise the advertisements to you.

Legal basis. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the consent form appears before the first advertisement. Personalised advertising, and the storing of identifiers for it, rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can refuse or withdraw at any time. The limited purposes the form lists as “legitimate interest” — showing basic, non-personalised advertisements, measuring them, and keeping the service secure and free of fraud — rest on our and Google's legitimate interest in funding and protecting a free game (Article 6(1)(f)); the form lets you object to those too. Elsewhere, processing takes place as local law permits, and the tracking prompt still applies.

Who receives it. Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in Europe, Google LLC elsewhere) acts as an independent controller of this data, and its certified advertising partners may receive parts of it to show and measure advertisements. The exact list of partners is shown inside the consent form, and Google publishes it as its ad technology providers. How Google uses the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy; how long it keeps it, in Google's retention practices.

Where it goes. Google processes data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For those transfers Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as described in Google's data transfer frameworks.

Ad attribution. Google may use Apple's SKAdNetwork to learn, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an advertisement led to an app being installed. That mechanism is designed by Apple to work without personal identifiers.

Every third party named on this page is bound, by its own published policies and by the terms under which it provides its service, to protect your data to at least the standard described here and required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

3. Purchases: Apple

Sway offers one purchase, Remove Ads, which is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy. What we receive from the transaction is whether the purchase is active on your device and a transaction identifier that does not identify you; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The legal basis is the purchase contract itself (Article 6(1)(b)).

Once Remove Ads is owned, the game no longer starts the advertising software, requests no advertisements, and does not show the tracking prompt.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks once, after your first level, whether Sway may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining changes nothing in the game; the advertisements simply become less personalised. Change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent. If the consent form applied to you, you can reopen it and change any choice from Privacy options on the game's settings screen. Withdrawing consent takes effect from then on; it does not undo processing that already happened lawfully.
  • Google's own controls. Google lets you see and manage the advertising data it holds at My Ad Center, whether or not you use a Google account.
  • Remove Ads. Buying it turns the advertising off entirely, as described above.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything the game itself keeps.

5. Children

Sway is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13, or under the age of digital consent where they live if that is higher — in Greece, 15. Advertisements are requested with a content rating suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, write to us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, and similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form; and, where processing rests on consent, to withdraw that consent at any time. Because the game holds none of your personal data, a request to us is usually answered by pointing you to the right control at Google or Apple, or by helping you use it — but write to us anyway if anything is unclear, and we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority; you may also complain to the authority where you live.

We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. The personalisation of advertisements, where you have consented to it, is done by Google as described above.
